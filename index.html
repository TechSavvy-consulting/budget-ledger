First month workflow

This app is a simple home bookkeeping system. Use it to keep one clean record of money coming in, money going out, where the money is, what you owe, and whether your records match the bank.

1. Add accounts Add checking, savings, credit cards, loans, and important assets. Enter each opening balance once.
2. Set targets Create budget categories for the things you want to watch, like Housing, Grocery, Fuel, and Insurance.
3. Enter activity Add paychecks, expenses, transfers, card purchases, and repeating bills as transactions.
4. Reconcile Compare each bank or credit card statement to the register, then lock matched items when the difference is $0.00.

First 10 Minutes

Walkthrough
1Open Accounts & Assets. Add Checking, Savings, and Credit Card. Use the current real-world balance as the opening balance.
2Open Budgets. Add targets for Housing, Grocery, Utilities, Fuel, Insurance, Dining, Savings, and Debt Payment.
3Open Home. Add your paycheck as Income to Checking. Add rent or groceries as Expenses from the account that paid them.
4For a credit card purchase, add an Expense to the credit card account. For a card payment, add a Transfer from Checking to Credit Card.
5Open Reports and Accounts & Assets weekly. At statement time, mark items seen at the bank and reconcile the account.

Home

Use Home for fast entry and a quick read on budget pressure. Quick Add is best for simple income, expenses, and transfers that do not need notes or splits.

  • Pick the month at the top before reviewing totals.
  • Use Quick Add for day-to-day entries.
  • Use Budget Categories sorted by Warnings and Upcoming Bills & Income to decide what needs attention next.

Transactions

Transactions are the main ledger. Use the date range fields to narrow what you are reviewing, and use Add Recurring for bills, paychecks, and transfers that repeat.

  • Expenses subtract from cash accounts or increase what you owe on credit cards.
  • Income adds money to the selected account.
  • Transfers move money between accounts and do not count as spending.

Budgets

Create targets for categories you want to track. Budget periods can be weekly, monthly, or yearly, and reports compare actual spending against those targets.

  • Use clear category names like Grocery, Housing, Fuel, or Insurance.
  • Edit a target when your plan changes.
  • Delete targets you no longer track.

Accounts & Assets

Add checking, savings, credit cards, loans, vehicles, and other assets or liabilities. The register shows running account activity inside the selected date range.

  • Check In Bank when a transaction appears at your bank.
  • Enter statement date and balance in Reconcile.
  • Reconcile only when the difference reaches $0.00.

Credit Cards & Loans

Record the thing you bought as the expense. Record the payment as a transfer. This prevents double-counting.

  • Grocery on card: Expense from Credit Card, category Grocery.
  • Card payment: Transfer from Checking to Credit Card.
  • Loan payment: split principal/interest if you need detailed tracking.

Reports

Reports summarize income, spending, remaining cash, net worth, daily expense flow, upcoming cash flow, top spending, and account balances for the selected period.

  • Switch Week, Month, or Year at the top.
  • Use Daily Expense Flow to spot unusual spending days.
  • Use Upcoming Cash Flow to see what is coming next.

Settings

Admins can add users, assign roles, reset passwords, edit books, delete books, and manage demo data. Standard users can change appearance, change their own password, and import or export their own book data.

  • Fresh installs start with Admin plus Kyle, Jessica, and Ohara user logins, each with a separate budget book.
  • Use Security to change the password for the account you are currently logged into.
  • Admins can reset another user's password by editing that user in the Users section.
  • Admins should keep at least one admin login active.
  • Users open only their own book and cannot see or edit other users' books.
  • Use Log Out when sharing a computer.

Import & Export

Use CSV Template to download the section-based import layout, Export CSV to download the current book in that same layout, Import CSV to add selected app sections, Import Spreadsheet for the original worksheet format, Export Spreadsheet to return matching data to that worksheet, or Backup App Folder for app files plus current data.

  • CSV files can include any mix of accounts, budgets, recurring items, and transactions by using the section column.
  • Export CSV matches the CSV Template columns, so it can be edited and imported back into the app.
  • Import Spreadsheet reads matching fields from the long-used budget worksheet and adds them to the current book.
  • Export Spreadsheet keeps the original workbook template, formulas, and formatting where possible.
  • Backup App Folder downloads the app files, worksheet template, and a JSON state file together.

Reconcile a Statement

Reconciliation proves your ledger matches the bank or credit card statement for a specific ending date.

  • Select the account and statement date.
  • Mark In Bank for every statement-cleared transaction.
  • Enter the statement balance and reconcile only when the difference is $0.00.
  • Reconciled transactions become locked history.

Common Mistakes

Most ledger problems come from double-counting, missing accounts, or importing over good data.

  • Do not enter a credit card payment as both an expense and a transfer.
  • Set opening balances once, then let transactions change balances.
  • Use transfers for savings moves and card payments.
  • Use Backup App Folder before importing or deleting anything important.
